II · The Lore

Every leaf is a letter
the earth wrote to us.

Long before botanists gave names to things, the world spoke in roots and petals. A plant was not a specimen — it was a pact. The lotus promised rebirth; the mandrake guarded the threshold; the world-tree held the ceiling of the sky so the stars would not fall.

Flora Mythica gathers these pacts into a single, breathing atlas — part herbarium, part confession. Read it slowly. The garden listens.
